Halachic innovation

Solving the Yom Tov Hot Water Challenge

For years, the use of tankless water heaters on Yom Tov has presented a major halachic challenge.

Tankless systems activate a fire immediately when a hot water faucet is opened. On Yom Tov, causing this direct ignition or heating cycle is a significant halachic issue for many poskim (halachic authorities).

Recognizing the need for a seamless, energy-efficient solution that fully respects halachic boundaries, our team at Chag Solutions set out to solve this problem.

We engaged in extensive, in-depth discussions with numerous leading Rabbis and halachic experts to fully map out the core issues as they apply to modern tankless technology. Guided by their invaluable insights and specific halachic parameters, our engineering team went to work.

The result is TETRIPH, a breakthrough system developed under the close, ongoing guidance of Rabbanim. TETRIPH creates a permitted framework for hot water usage on Yom Tov for Ochel Nefesh, entirely eliminating the issue of direct, user-triggered ignition. Our solution bridges the gap between modern convenience and uncompromising halachic integrity, giving Jewish homes peace of mind without sacrificing comfort.
